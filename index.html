Settings & backups

Offline storage

Workout records are saved on this device. Data does not automatically sync to other devices. Export a backup regularly so you don't lose your history if Safari storage is cleared or you change phones.

Install on iPhone

Open the GitHub Pages URL in Safari, tap Share, and choose Add to Home Screen. Open Gym Log once online to cache it for offline use.
